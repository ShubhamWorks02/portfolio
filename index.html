Architecture showcase

NuConnect

NuConnect is a production BLE hearing-aid companion platform supporting 5+ device families from one capability-abstracted mobile architecture. Hardware differences are modeled at runtime instead of patched through platform-level rewrites.

Product flows

  • Config Assistant for 16+ DSP channel tuning beyond EQ.
  • Remote Fitting with cloud profiles applied through live GATT writes.
  • Dynamic support for 6+ hardware models.

Impact

  • BLE communication latency reduced by roughly 70%.
  • Pairing time reduced from 10s to 4s.
  • Command acknowledgment reliability reached 99.2%.

Clinical outcomes

  • Audiologist visits reduced by roughly 30%.
  • Average fitting time moved from 45 minutes to under 15 minutes.
  • Clinic revisit rates reduced by roughly 25%.

Architecture

  • MVVM, Hilt, Room, model-driven capability abstraction.
  • Environment-aware Q&A flow normalizing sharp whistle feedback through channel suppression.
